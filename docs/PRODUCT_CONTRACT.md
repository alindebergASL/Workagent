# Product direction — active build mode

Andrew's October 16, 2026 direction: “can we blow up these contracts, we are still
in build mode and not prod”. This replaces accumulated production-style gates and
historical slice restrictions. The product ambition remains unchanged.

Build a general, persistent work partner: understand intent, use permitted context
and tools, make useful things, adapt to observations, coordinate work when helpful,
and own responsibilities across time and interruption.

- Ordinary conversation works from Home. No project, workflow form, internal ID
  or operator-prepared conversation is required to begin.
- The agent chooses useful next actions. Retaining a draft does not inherently
  end the task. Bounded executions continue the same durable responsibility.
- Tools perform real work. Methods/skills are reusable guidance; neither a demo
  nor a specific business workflow defines the supported task menu.
- Ongoing work can revise its next action, wait for a meaningful event, check in,
  recover, or establish an evidence-backed outcome within standing authority.
- Preserve human contributions and make consequential changes easy to understand,
  accept, reject, undo or steer. Routine preparation need not ask for approval.
- Use scoped recall and develop memory/learning incrementally through [#10](https://github.com/alindebergASL/Workagent/issues/10).
  The entire learning backlog is not a prerequisite for useful ongoing work.

## Experience

Follow the [approved alpha concepts](design/alpha-intelligent-ui-20261015/README.md)
for hierarchy, density and interaction. Home shows relevant work, outcomes and
decisions. Named work is easy to find and resume; conversation stays beside it.
Source editing, history and machinery are secondary. Real uncertainty should be
clear and actionable rather than repeated as generic disclaimers.

Choose concise text, editable work, comparisons, charts or generated interactive
views according to the task. Sandboxed HTML/CSS/JS is a valid capability.
Controls must operate on real authorized state. Preserve a stable shell and active
inputs. No separate lending, supply or GTD product architecture is required.

## Development freedom

Owners may refactor or replace development interfaces, schemas, runtime profiles,
authorization implementation and architecture. Keep the protections they serve;
do not preserve incidental demo restrictions or every legacy fixture behavior.
Use migrations or isolated environments when needed to protect retained work.

Eight-turn lifetime limits, prelisted demo conversations, first-draft stop rules,
blanket human review after routine continuation, and finite showcase verifiers
are not the general product specification. Remove or generalize these limitations
in implementation. This document does not itself change installed authority.
