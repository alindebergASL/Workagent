# Product direction

The canonical [product blueprint](product/01-product-blueprint.md) defines the complete
P01–P16 scope: general delegation, native editable work, connected sources and artifacts,
partner/generated apps, ongoing responsibilities, memory, media and cross-device continuity.
The [architecture](product/02-architectural-plan.md) describes implementation boundaries;
the [build steps](product/03-build-steps.md) schedule concrete delivery.

Start naturally from conversation. Keep it available beside real editable artifacts.
Preserve human contributions, show useful progress and decisions, and keep machinery in
optional details. A fresh client replaces the rejected experience; historical alpha
concepts and the workshop do not prescribe its UI or runtime.

First deliver a real unfamiliar-input result in a sheet or document, human correction
and useful continuation. Bring interruption recovery and closed-client responsibilities
forward before broad suite completion. This sequence preserves every named capability;
missing integrations remain incomplete and cannot support a full-parity claim.

[Development direction](DEVELOPMENT_AUTHORITY.md) defines the currently authorized scope,
[model spending](GENERAL_MODEL_AUTHORIZATION.md) controls spend, and [STATUS](../STATUS.md)
records what actually ran. Product direction itself does not authorize implementation or
effects. See [working boundaries](WORK_CONTRACT.md) and [evaluation](PRODUCT_ACCEPTANCE.md)
for the retained behavioral principles.
