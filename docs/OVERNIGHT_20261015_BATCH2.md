## What changed for a person

An explicit human decision no longer disappears because somebody asks an unrelated
question, evidence changes, or a check-in becomes due. The responsibility keeps
its exact wait, next action and current outcome. Material changes can flag review,
but cannot invent the person's decision or queue provider work. An explicit
human release can resume one authorized controlled continuation, without declaring
the objective complete. Pause/resume and restart preserve that boundary.

Claude's frontend now opens the chosen work once, in place. It runs a saved
interactive view inside the existing sandbox and exact-version read broker,
with navigation to its canonical inputs and the views using an input. A supplier
investigation exercises a meaningfully different representation from an event
plan. Changing a saved input invalidates the comparison; a slider is explicitly
unsaved and does not become a backend mutation or model call. Human document-note
editing, canonical saves and reload are exercised again.

The investigation's data, view shape and provider decisions are AUTHORED fixtures.
Production rendering/binding is generic and not keyed to benchmark prompts, but
this is NOT evidence a live model selected or adapted a useful representation.
The user's intelligence bar remains open; another deterministic comparison is not
acceptance of general agency or an intelligent UI.
