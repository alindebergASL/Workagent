# Small intake experiment
A sample is descriptive, not representative. Deduplicate by stable case ID.
Choose one change to test on the next bounded batch; compare recorded completeness.
Capture elapsed time separately before claiming time savings. Avoid causal conclusions.
Label proposed work separately from saved human decisions; a completeness percentage is
descriptive evidence, not acceptance or proof of a successful intervention.
A checklist should let a person act without rerunning the agent. Unresolved cases keep
an explicit next action and responsible person to confirm, never an invented assignment.
