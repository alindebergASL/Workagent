Synthetic rainwater planning exercise. Build and save a reusable calculator for collected water, not just a written answer. Inputs: roof area in whole square metres, rainfall in whole millimetres, and capture efficiency as a whole percent from 0 to 100. Output whole litres, rounded down: roof area times rainfall times efficiency divided by 100. Start with 80 square metres, 12 millimetres and 75 percent. Check a zero-rainfall case and a fractional-litre case too. Label units so a person can reuse it. All inputs are synthetic. Do not claim independent verification from your own explanation or execute external actions.
