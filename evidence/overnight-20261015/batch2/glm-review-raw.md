# Critique of WorkAgent Overnight Progress (candidate 12c3e3)

## Engineering — genuine advances

The backend work is the strongest material. General bounded saved-work discovery/read with exact revision/hash and a 32,768-byte aggregate limit directly fixes the live failure mode (the 8,192-byte projection omission that orphaned the 10,215-byte workshop plan). Immutable read receipts, CAS/pending-only mutation, byte-exact preservation of protected human notes in proposals, and independently reviewed tests plus 5 new ones are real representation-and-integrity changes, not polish. The human-wait rework — preserving exact wait/action/outcome deltas, evidence flagging review without granting permission, explicit release for exactly one controlled continuation, restart/pause/resume tested — is a meaningful ownership mechanic. The 15 controlled React Chromium permission cases closing prior completed-request races are solid, with honest scoping of the two pending-sibling cases as client retention P2, not an authz bypass.

Weaknesses: no natural-language decision interpretation, no live autonomy test, full backend suite still running with mixed source window, no final frontend reapproval for the in-place view delta. The permission model remains "CAS/pending proposals only" — sound but narrow.

## Usefulness — partial

The revision-recovery fix addresses a real user harm from the original evaluation (the failed ordinary revision). Waiting records becoming generic runnable reviews is a step from passive log toward actionable state. The supplier-evidence comparison demo is materially different from the prior event plan and runs production components in the same sandbox/read-only broker — consistent architecture.

But the evidence explicitly says the comparison demo's content, layout, and provider decisions are hand-authored fixtures. It is not model-generated and not proof of intelligent UI. Local sliders don't save or run models. The user's demand for broad general agency, extensible skills/tools, durable continuity, and authorized initiative remains largely unmet: memory is scoped editable notes without export/forget/provenance; product-model calls are zero; autonomy is one controlled continuation, never tested live.

## Experience — limited, honestly labeled

The visible slice binds canonical work via Reads/Used-by rather than scenario titles, and unbind-on-human-change (invalidate view until rebound) is a correct trust behavior. Beyond that, experience claims are thin and the report itself concedes the "intelligent UI" surface is untested with a live model. No original-preview regression is claimed; nothing is invented beyond what fixtures show.

## Old live failures vs. new controlled proof

Cleanly separated: the six admissions (explanation/return answered, untargeted revision failure, no human safety note ever saved, partial waiting responsibility, READY-as-answer) are retained unchanged and not re-litigated. New evidence is controlled tests and fixtures only — appropriate caution. Importantly, no browser action or human safety note is retroactively credited to the original evaluation.

## NanoMuse trajectory?

Partially converging. Correction-priority: yes, structurally (exact revisions, byte-exact protected notes, invalidation-on-change). Tool/context ladder: the read broker, bounded discovery, and same-sandbox in-place execution approximate it. Persistent continuity: only a scoped waiting record plus conversation-scoped discovery — not durable cross-session memory. Selective initiative: the single released continuation is a floor, not selective initiative. Generated surfaces: absent; the comparison view is authored, not generated. Verdict: infrastructure for that architecture is being laid correctly, but the agentive and generative halves are unbuilt. No parity claim versus Muse/dots/Grok/Hermes is made or warranted; none should be.

## Non-credits (per instructions)

- The waiting record is not ownership; waiting ≠ achieved objective.
- The comparison demo is a test demo, not model intelligence.
- Scoped editable notes are not complete persistent memory.
- No fabricated browser actions or safety notes in the original evaluation.

## Four highest-value acceptance probes

1. **Live targeted revision recovery**: replay the original failed workshop-plan revision on the live system — does bounded discovery surface it, preserve protected notes byte-exact, and produce a user-accepted revision end-to-end?
2. **One released continuation with a live model**: exercise the human-release path under a real decision scenario, verifying evidence flags review without granting permission and the delta remains exact across restart.
3. **Memory boundaries probe**: attempt cross-session recall beyond the scoped note (provenance, export, forget) to establish what continuity actually persists and what it does not.
4. **Mutation-path completeness on the in-place view**: full frontend reapproval plus adversarial attempts to mutate work outside Ask/Review/Edit through the in-place custom view, including the pending-sibling race class.

## Budget integrity

Consistent and conservative: 40 cumulative, 24.6 encumbered, draft-only 2-admission envelope not installed, clone grants inactive, unknowns quarantined, zero generation calls. No accounting anomalies.

**Overall**: genuine, well-tested infrastructure progress in correction priority and controlled mutation; no yet-evidenced progress on general agency, persistent memory, or genuinely intelligent UI. Moving in the right direction at the plumbing layer only.
